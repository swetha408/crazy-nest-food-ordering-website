Orders

How do I track my order?
Once your order is placed, you'll receive a confirmation with an order ID. You can track your order in real-time by going to My Orders section. Our delivery partner will also send SMS updates.
Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 minutes of placing them. After that, our kitchen starts preparing your food and changes cannot be made.
What if an item is missing from my order?
Please report missing items within 30 minutes of delivery using the "Report an Issue" option in your order history. We'll arrange a replacement or refund promptly.

Payment

What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery. All online payments are secured with 256-bit encryption.
My payment failed but money was deducted. What do I do?
Failed transaction amounts are automatically reversed within 5–7 business days. If you don't receive a refund, please contact your bank or reach out to our support team.
Is it safe to save my card details?
Yes. We use PCI-DSS compliant tokenization — your full card details are never stored on our servers. Only a secure token is saved for faster future checkouts.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery time is 30–45 minutes depending on your location and store traffic. During peak hours it may take slightly longer.
What is the delivery charge?
Orders above ₹499 get free delivery. A convenience fee of ₹30–₹50 may apply for orders below this amount.
My order hasn't arrived. What should I do?
Check the live tracking in your order history. If you can't reach the delivery partner, contact our support via the chat option below.

Refunds

How do I request a refund?
Go to My Orders → Select Order → Report Issue and choose "Request Refund". Our team will review and process it within 48 hours.
How long does a refund take?
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. UPI refunds are usually faster (1–2 days).

My Account

How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Enter your registered email or mobile number and we'll send a reset link or OTP within a minute.
How do I update my delivery address?
Go to Account → Saved Addresses to add, edit, or remove delivery addresses. You can also add a new address at checkout.

Offers & Coupons

How do I apply a coupon code?
On the checkout page, tap "Apply Coupon" and enter your code. The discount will automatically be applied if the order meets the minimum value requirement.
Why is my coupon not working?
The coupon may have expired, your order total may be below the minimum required value, or the coupon is valid for a specific outlet only. Check the offer terms or contact support.

Our support team is available 9 AM – 11 PM, 7 days a week.
